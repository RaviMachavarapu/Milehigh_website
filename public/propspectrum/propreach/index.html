BRAND VOICE SPEC, EXAMPLE

TONE

Warm but direct. Knowledgeable without being condescending. Confident, never pushy. Writes like a trusted advisor, not a salesperson.

PHRASES TO USE

"Here's what I'm seeing right now" · "What this means for you" · "In my experience" · First-name sign-offs

PHRASES TO AVOID

"Dream home" · "Don't miss out" · "Act now" · Anything that sounds like a billboard

CONTENT PILLARS

Market education · Personal story · Client wins (anonymised) · Relocation tips · Community knowledge

PropReach, common questions

The things agents ask before they sign up.

"How do I know it will actually sound like me?"

add

Before a single post is written, we run a brand voice session with you, 30–45 minutes where we capture your tone, your phrases, your market perspective, and what you'd never say. We build a written spec from that session. Every piece of content is written against that spec and reviewed by our team before it enters your queue. If it doesn't sound like you, it gets rewritten. You also have final approval before anything goes live.

"Do I have to log into social media to post?"

add

No. We connect your social accounts during onboarding, and approved content posts automatically on the set schedule. You see the calendar, you approve the content, and the system handles publishing across LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook. The only interaction you have is approving, which takes a few minutes whenever the content lands in your queue.

"I want to do video. Does PropReach help with that?"

add

Yes. PropReach includes video script writing as part of the content engine, short-form scripts for Reels, TikTok-style clips, neighbourhood spotlights, and market takes. We write the script, structure the hook, and have it ready when you want to record. We also write talking points for longer-form content like podcasts or YouTube videos. You bring the camera and the personality. We handle everything else.

"Can PropReach handle my past client outreach too?"

add

Yes, and this is one of the most underused parts of the product. PropReach includes past client nurture content: birthday messages, closing anniversaries, seasonal check-ins, and market updates tailored to their specific neighbourhood. These are written personally and sent automatically. Your past clients feel remembered. Referrals follow naturally from consistent, genuine outreach, not from a blast email they ignore.

"How much content do I actually get per month?"

add

Scope is set during the audit based on your goals and which platforms matter most. At the entry tier: 8 posts per month + listing content. Mid tier: 16 posts + email newsletter. Full tier: complete content engine including video scripts, past client outreach, and referral content. Pricing is scoped to your specific situation, the free audit is where we map it out.
