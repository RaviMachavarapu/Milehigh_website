FAQ

Questions about PropOptics

The most common things agents ask us before they see their first result.

"Will buyers be able to tell it's AI-staged?"

+

Not with PropOptics. This is exactly why we have a designer review step. Generic AI staging can look off, furniture floats, shadows don't match, proportions are wrong. Our designers correct all of that before delivery. The final image should be indistinguishable from a high-end physical staging shoot.

"Is there a disclosure requirement for AI staging?"

+

MLS rules vary by market, but most require a disclosure when photos are digitally staged, the same applies to physical staging photos. We provide properly labeled versions of all staged images for MLS compliance. Your MHAL account manager knows the local requirements and can advise.

"What if I don't like the first version?"

+

Revisions are included. You can request a style change, a different furniture palette, or specific adjustments. Because our designer is already in the loop, changes are usually turned around within a few hours, not another full production cycle.

"Does it work on occupied listings?"

+

Yes. For occupied listings, we use photo enhancement rather than full staging, we can remove clutter, adjust lighting, improve the sky, and clean up distracting elements while keeping the existing furniture and feel of the space intact. This is one of our most-used services.

"I only have a few listings a month. Is it worth it?"

+

If your listings sell faster and command stronger prices because the visual marketing is premium, PropOptics pays for itself on the first transaction. Most agents doing 1–2 listings/month find the monthly plan covers multiple listing packages, you're staging every listing, not just the expensive ones.

"Can I use PropOptics with my current photographer?"

+

Absolutely. A lot of agents use PropOptics to extend their photographer's work, the photographer shoots, we stage the empty rooms and enhance any exterior shots. Your photographer focuses on what they do best; we handle the digital production layer on top.
