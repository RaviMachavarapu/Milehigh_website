Common questions about PropLeads

Things agents ask before getting started.

What CRM does PropLeads work with?

expand_more

We configure PropLeads around your existing CRM, Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, LionDesk, HubSpot, Go High Level, and others. If you don't have one, we include setup as part of the build. The stack is determined during the audit call.

Do I need to import all my contacts manually?

expand_more

No. You export your contact list from wherever it currently lives, spreadsheet, email, old CRM, and send it to us. We handle the import, cleaning, segmentation, and scoring. You don't touch it again until the approval review.

What if my contacts are a mess?

expand_more

That's the most common starting point. Duplicates, outdated info, missing fields, we clean all of it as part of the data intake. The pipeline you see at approval looks nothing like what you exported. That's the point.

Is this a one-time setup or ongoing?

expand_more

Ongoing managed service. Monthly retainer includes pipeline health reviews, scoring updates, new contact imports, and a monthly check-in. When your business changes, new lead sources, new CRM features, we adapt the pipeline with you.
