## 01 — What PropSpectrum Actually Is

PropSpectrum is the real estate wing of Mile High AI Labs — a **done-for-you AI managed service** where the team's expertise, not any single tool, is what the client is buying.

The distinction that matters: anyone can subscribe to the same AI tools we use. What they cannot replicate is how we combine them. MHAL brings genuine AI expertise and a canonical, repeatable process — a structured method for selecting, configuring, and orchestrating the right tools to produce a specific, reliable output for each client. The tools are instruments. The process is the product.

Think of it like a recording studio. A client doesn't care which microphones or mixing board are in the room. They care that every time they walk in, a professional result comes out. Our canonical process is the studio. Human-in-the-Loop is the engineer who checks the mix before it leaves the building.

**The canonical process — six steps:**
1. **Diagnose** — map the client's current workflow, identify the specific gap (slow lead response, empty pipeline, no content, bad visuals)
2. **Select** — identify which AI tools best solve that gap for that specific client. No two implementations use the same stack
3. **Configure** — build and connect the tools: automations, sequences, integrations, content pipelines, visual workflows
4. **Review (HITL)** — every output is reviewed by a human team member before it reaches the client's business
5. **Deploy** — go live with monitoring. Watch the first 48 hours.
6. **Maintain** — ongoing managed service. Monthly check-ins, tuning, and iteration

**The company in one sentence:**
PropSpectrum applies Mile High AI Labs' AI expertise and canonical delivery process to real estate — so agents get reliable, reviewed results without building, learning, or managing anything themselves.

**Three words:** Expert. Process. Accountable.

**What it is NOT:**
- Not a virtual staging company (PropOptics is one product inside PropSpectrum)
- Not a SaaS tool agents self-serve
- Not a consulting engagement with a deliverable and an end date
- Not another AI chrome extension, lead scraper, or ChatGPT wrapper
- Not a company where AI runs unsupervised and errors reach clients unchecked

## 02 — The Name

**PropSpectrum** = Property + Full Spectrum of Solutions.

"Prop" anchors the brand in real estate without explanation. "Spectrum" communicates range — not one tool, not one problem, but the full operating layer a modern real estate team needs.

**How to always write it:** PropSpectrum — one word, capital P, capital S. Never "Prop Spectrum."

**How to always say it:** "Prop-Spectrum" — two clean syllables each.

**The competitive distinction:** PropStream = data and lead lists for investors. PropSpectrum = managed AI operations for agents and teams. Different buyer, different problem, different category.

**"By Mile High AI Labs"** appears in the footer of propspectrum.ai and in the email signature — establishes the parent relationship without putting MHAL's broader identity in front of the real estate brand.

## 03 — The Audience

### Primary ICP — Real Estate Agents and Small Teams

**Who exactly:**
Individual agents and small teams (2–5 people) doing 20+ transactions per year in the Denver metro area and surrounding Colorado markets. Producing enough to feel the operational pain but not large enough to have a dedicated ops person.

**What they are doing right now:**
- Responding to leads hours or days late — and losing them to whoever responded first
- Manually following up across text, email, and phone with no system
- Spending evenings on social content, listing marketing, and admin
- Using one or two point tools that don't talk to each other
- Hearing about AI constantly but not knowing what to actually implement

**What they believe that is wrong:**
- "AI tools are complicated and I don't have time to learn them"
- "I tried a CRM and it didn't stick"
- "My clients want a human, not a bot"
- "I can't afford to hire anyone right now"

**What they need to hear:**
You don't set it up. You don't run it. You don't review it. We do all of that. You get the result.

**What makes them say yes:**
A specific, believable before-and-after — not "AI will transform your business." The stat that converts: "An agent in Centennial went from 917-minute average lead response to under 5 minutes, without changing anything about how she works."

### Secondary ICP — Real Estate Teams and Brokerages
Team leads and brokerages with 5–20 agents who need to standardize operations across the team. Higher deal value, longer sales cycle, needs a deck and a discovery call. Not the focus for the first 90 days — but the website architecture does not exclude them.

## 05 — Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) — The Positioning Core

HITL is not a feature. It is what makes the canonical process trustworthy. Anyone can run AI tools. PropSpectrum's team knows which tools produce which outputs — and a human reviews every result before it reaches a client.

**The HITL argument in plain English:**
Every AI tool on the market runs on autopilot. It generates an email, sends it. It builds an automation, deploys it. No one checks it. When it goes wrong — and it goes wrong — it goes wrong in front of your clients. PropSpectrum puts a human between the AI and the output every single time.

**The HITL 4-step visual flow (used on every product page):**
This is a design pattern that appears as a dedicated section on each product page. Four cards in sequence:

1. **Lead / Request Arrives** — new contact, inquiry, or content request enters the system
2. **AI Drafts** — PropAI, PropLeads, PropOptics, or PropReach generates the first output
3. **MHAL Team Reviews** *(highlighted in Terracotta — this is the differentiator step)* — a human checks the output for accuracy, tone, brand fit, and quality before anything is sent or published
4. **Lead / Result Delivered** — the reviewed, approved output reaches the client's business

This four-step flow is present on every product page as a section. The MHAL Reviews card always gets the terracotta/accent border treatment to visually anchor the differentiator.

> **Note — PropReach variation:** PropReach expands the flow to 5 steps: Brand Voice Session → Content Generated → MHAL Team Reviews (terracotta) → You Approve → Posts Automatically. The additional steps reflect PropReach's unique approval model where the client sees content before it publishes.

**HITL per product:**

| Product | What Gets Reviewed |
|---------|-------------------|
| PropAI | Every automation logic reviewed before go-live; every drafted message reviewed before send |
| PropLeads | CRM data and lead scoring logic validated by team before client sees pipeline |
| PropOptics | Every visual approved by a designer before delivery to client |
| PropReach | All content reviewed by MHAL team before entering approval queue — agent approves, system posts |

**HITL in the sales conversation:**
When a prospect says "I can just use an AI tool myself" — the response is: "You can. But when it produces the wrong output, who catches it before it goes to a client? We do. That's what you're paying for."

**HITL on the website:**
It appears in the hero section or immediately below it on every product page — not buried in an about section. It is the differentiator, not a footnote.

## 09 — Mobile and Navigation (LOCKED)

**Decision:** Full-screen mobile hamburger nav overlay on all five pages.

**Implementation:**
- `<nav>` has `z-index: 40` on all pages
- Mobile menu overlay is `fixed inset-0 bg-midnight-navy z-50` — covers the entire screen including the nav
- Overlay starts `display:none`, toggled to `display:flex` by a hamburger button
- Hamburger button: `<button class="md:hidden" onclick="document.getElementById('mobile-menu').style.display='flex'">`
- Close button inside overlay sets `display:none`
- All nav links inside the overlay also close the menu when tapped (inline onclick)
- Desktop CTA ("Book Free Audit") is `hidden md:inline-flex` so only the hamburger shows on mobile

**Why this approach:**
No external JS dependencies. Inline onclick is reliable across all browsers without a build step. The midnight-navy full-screen overlay provides clear visual focus and maintains the brand palette on mobile.

## 12 — Social Media Context

### The distinction: PropReach automates the posting, not just the creation
PropSpectrum's own social media (what the brand posts to build its audience) follows the three pillars below. This is separate from PropReach, which automates social posting for clients. When selling PropReach: "We generate the content, review it, and post it automatically — you just approve what goes out."

### The three content pillars for PropSpectrum's own channels:

**Pillar 1 — Education (40% of content)**
Teach real estate agents something useful about AI without selling to them.
Examples: "The 5-minute rule: why lead response time is the only metric that matters," "What a CRM actually does (and why most agents never use theirs)," "3 things AI can automate in your real estate business this week."
Tone: Direct, no jargon, written for a busy agent scrolling LinkedIn between showings.

**Pillar 2 — Proof (35% of content)**
Show the work. Show the outputs. Before-and-after.
Examples: PropOptics renders (before listing photo → after AI staging), Apollo sequence results (anonymized), workflow diagrams of PropAI automations, PropReach content calendar screenshots.
Tone: Let the visual carry the post. Short caption. The image does the work.

**Pillar 3 — POV (25% of content)**
Nethya and the team's perspective on what is happening in real estate AI — not neutral, not corporate.
Examples: "Virtual staging is becoming a commodity — here's what agents actually need now," "Why we built a Human-in-the-Loop model when everyone else went full automation," "The honest answer to 'can I just use ChatGPT for this?'"
Tone: First-person, confident, willing to take a position.

### Platforms and priority:
- **LinkedIn** — Primary. Agents, brokers, team leaders. Every piece of content publishes here first.
- **Instagram** — Visual proof content. PropOptics before-and-afters, behind-the-scenes.
- **Facebook** — Passive distribution. Same content as Instagram. Not a primary creation platform.

### PropSpectrum runs on PropReach:
PropSpectrum's own content calendar, posting schedule, and cross-platform distribution is built and managed using the same PropReach stack sold to clients. This is both operationally efficient and a genuine proof point: "We use this ourselves."

### What is off-brand for social:
- Hype language ("Revolutionizing real estate!" / "Game-changing AI!")
- Content that positions PropSpectrum as a tool rather than a managed service
- Anything that could be mistaken for PropStream content (data-heavy, investor-focused)
- Vanity metrics posts (follower counts, likes milestones)

## 14 — Client Onboarding Flow (12 Days)

**Step 1 — Free AI Workflow Audit (Day 0)**
30-minute discovery call. Map current workflow, identify the specific gap, show where PropSpectrum plugs in. No pitch deck. Delivers value whether or not they proceed. CTA on every page.

**Step 2 — Scope and Proposal (Days 1–2)**
Within 48 hours: one-page scope. Which product(s), what is built, what is needed from them (CRM access, brand assets, logins), and pricing. Client signs off. Short — no 40-page proposal.

**Step 3 — Kickoff and Access (Day 3)**
20-minute kickoff call. Collect access credentials. Client does not need to be technical. This is the last time they need to be actively involved until the review call.

**Step 4 — Build Phase (Days 4–10)**
MHAL team works. Automations, sequences, content, visuals — all handled internally. Client gets one status update midway.

**Step 5 — HITL Review and Approval (Days 10–11)**
MHAL team has already reviewed every output internally. Client sees the final result, approves, requests any changes. This is the Human-in-the-Loop moment that justifies the retainer.

**Step 6 — Go-Live (Day 12)**
Everything goes live. MHAL monitors the first 48 hours and catches anything that needs tuning before the client notices.

**Step 7 — 2-Week Check-in and Monthly Rhythm**
Check-in call at day 26. Monthly from there. This is the ongoing managed service relationship — not a one-time project.

- Your market context — neighbourhood knowledge, price ranges, client types you serve
- Your story — what drives you, your background, what makes you different from every other agent
- Your non-negotiables — the phrases, the posture, the things you'd never put your name on

**Brand Voice Spec example card:**

| Category | Content |
|----------|---------|
| TONE | Warm but direct. Knowledgeable without being condescending. Confident, never pushy. Writes like a trusted advisor, not a salesperson. |
| PHRASES TO USE | "Here's what I'm seeing right now" · "What this means for you" · "In my experience" · First-name sign-offs |
| PHRASES TO AVOID | "Dream home" · "Don't miss out" · "Act now" · Anything that sounds like a billboard |
| CONTENT PILLARS | Market education · Personal story · Client wins (anonymised) · Relocation tips · Community knowledge |

#### CONTENT TYPES (6 cards — "Not just listing posts. Your full content engine.")

**Section headline:** Not just listing posts. Your full content engine.

**1. Market Content** (`trending_up` icon, amber-gold)
> Weekly or bi-weekly market updates — inventory, pricing trends, what's moving and what isn't. Written for your specific market, not national headlines. Positions you as the local expert your clients call first.
> *Platforms: LinkedIn, Email, Facebook*

**2. Personal Brand Content** (`person_heart` icon, amber-gold)
> Content that lets people get to know you — your background, how you work, what you believe about real estate, the moments that shaped your career. The posts that make strangers feel like they already know you before they reach out.
> *Platforms: Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook*

**3. Video Scripts** (`videocam` icon, terracotta)
> Short-form video scripts written in your voice — market takes, buyer/seller tips, neighbourhood spotlights, personal Q&As. You record. We write the script, structure the hook, and give you the talking points. Also includes podcast talking point outlines.
> *Platforms: Instagram Reels, Podcast, YouTube*

**4. Past Client Nurture** (`favorite` icon, deep-forest)
> Birthday messages, closing anniversaries, seasonal check-ins, market updates tailored to their neighbourhood — content designed to make your past clients feel remembered. Written personally. Sent automatically. Your past clients feel remembered because they are.
> *Platforms: Email, SMS, Social*

**5. Listing Marketing** (`home_work` icon, midnight-navy)
> Property description, listing highlights, just-listed posts, open house announcements, and sold story posts — all generated from the listing details and posted automatically across platforms. No copy-paste, no formatting, no scheduling.
> *Platforms: All platforms, Email blast*

**6. Referral & Repeat Content** (`share` icon, amber-gold)
> Content designed to remind your sphere you work in their network — relocation resources, dual-role positioning (Realtor + lender where applicable), community spotlight posts, and gentle referral asks built into the content calendar at the right intervals.
> *Platforms: LinkedIn, Email, SMS*

#### FAQ (5 questions)

**Q1: How do I know it will actually sound like me?**
> Because the first thing we do is learn how you communicate — not from a template, but from a conversation. We run a brand voice session before we write a single word: how you greet people, what you'd never say, the phrases that feel like you, the market angles you actually care about. Everything we write is checked against that profile by a human on our team before it ever gets to you. And you approve everything before it posts. If something doesn't sound right — tell us. We rewrite it.

**Q2: Do I have to log into social media to post?**
> No. During onboarding, we connect your accounts to the posting system. Once content is approved — by you — it posts automatically on the scheduled date and time. You can see the calendar. You never have to log in to schedule anything.

**Q3: I want to do video. Does PropReach help with that?**
> Yes. PropReach includes short-form video scripts written for Instagram Reels and TikTok-style clips — hook, talking points, and close, in your voice. You record, we handle everything else. For agents doing podcasts or YouTube, we also write talking point outlines so you're never walking in cold.

**Q4: Can PropReach handle my past client outreach too?**
> Yes. Past client nurture is one of the six content types PropReach manages. Birthday messages, closing anniversaries, seasonal check-ins, market updates tailored to their specific neighbourhood — all written in your voice, reviewed by our team, and sent automatically. Your past clients hear from you without you having to remember to reach out.

**Q5: How much content do I actually get per month?**
> It depends on the tier — which gets scoped at the audit. At entry level: 8 posts per month across LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook, plus listing content as it comes in. At mid tier: 16 posts plus a monthly email newsletter. At the full tier: a complete content engine including video scripts, past client campaigns, and referral sequences. All tiers include the brand voice session and MHAL team review on every piece.
