Common questions about PropAI

Things agents ask before getting started.

Will my leads know it's AI responding?

expand_more

No, and that's by design. Before we build anything, we do a voice session where we learn exactly how you communicate: your greetings, your language, what you'd never say. Every reply is matched to sound like you. Our clients' leads regularly comment that the follow-up felt personal and attentive.

What if the AI sends something wrong?

expand_more

That's what the HITL process prevents. Every outbound message is reviewed by our team before it sends. If a draft is off-brand, factually wrong, or not up to standard, it gets rewritten before it ever reaches a lead or client. This is not autopilot.

Which CRM does it work with?

expand_more

We build around your existing CRM, Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, LionDesk, HubSpot, Go High Level, and others. If you don't have a CRM, we'll include setup in the build. The stack we select is part of the audit conversation.

Is this a one-time setup or ongoing?

expand_more

Ongoing managed service. Monthly fee includes keeping an eye on things, a real person checking the work, adjusting the follow-ups as your business changes, and a monthly check-in call. When something needs fixing, we fix it, you hear about it after it's resolved.
